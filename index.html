AetherXR is architected strictly under zero-knowledge and zero-biometric constraints. We never access headset cameras, microphones, or raw iris data. Here is our verifiable technical breakdown of what is measured vs. what is strictly blocked.

What We Access (Legitimate Spatial Telemetry)
  • Head Rig Rigid Transform (Orientation Vector)

    Calculates whether the sponsored virtual object is positioned within the standard 60-degree central field-of-view.

  • Raycaster Controller Click Events

    Registers voluntary user clicks on 3D objects (e.g. changing the color of the showcase drone).

  • Session Timestamp & Browser Active Window State

    Tracks contiguous active session duration to prevent automated background tab farming.

What We NEVER Access (Strictly Prohibited)
  • Passthrough Cameras or Room Scans

    WebXR standards sandbox cameras entirely. Web applications have 0% access to your physical living room.

    No audio recording or voice telemetry is ever requested, captured, or transmitted.

  • Raw Eye-Tracking / Biometric Pupilometry

    We reject intrusive iris surveillance. Attention is estimated solely via coarse head orientation.
